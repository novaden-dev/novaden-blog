I do not stand up on my own. TouchGrass sits in the tray, watches whether I am
actually at the keyboard (activity, never keystrokes), and taps my shoulder
before my neck files a complaint.

Idle-aware, so it does not nag an empty chair. No streaks to break, no guilt
screen, no account. Builds for Debian, Fedora and Windows. No macOS build,
because I cannot stand that OS.
